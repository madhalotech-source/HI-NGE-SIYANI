# Project Mission

## Mission Statement

To remove the information barriers that stand between South Africans and the opportunities meant for them — by building one trusted, multilingual AI companion that guides every citizen, in their own language, through education, government services, and beyond.

## Our Commitments

**We build for access, not just innovation.**
Every feature is judged by whether it makes an opportunity easier to reach for someone who previously couldn't reach it.

**We respect the languages people already speak.**
Siyani meets citizens in isiZulu, Sepedi, Tshivenda, Xitsonga, and English today, with a clear roadmap to all eleven official languages — not as a translation layer bolted on, but as a first-class design requirement.

**We tell the truth about uncertainty.**
When Siyani isn't confident it understood a request — in any language — it asks a clarifying question rather than guessing. Trust is a core part of the product, not an afterthought.

**We protect personal information as a default, not a feature.**
Consent, minimal data collection, user-controlled deletion, and POPIA alignment are non-negotiable from day one — detailed in the [Security Strategy](16-security-strategy.md).

**We build realistically, one honest phase at a time.**
Every feature in our documentation is either working today or clearly labelled **Coming Soon**. We do not overstate our progress to judges, users, or partners.

## Who We Serve

Learners and students. Jobseekers and entrepreneurs. Parents and pensioners. Anyone who has ever given up looking for help because the system was too scattered, too complex, or too unfamiliar in language and tone.
