# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Nomvula, 19, Matric Graduate

**Province:** Limpopo
**Status:** Recent matric graduate, unsure of next step
**Home language:** Xitsonga

Nomvula passed matric but doesn't know which bursaries she qualifies for, or the difference between a TVET college and university application process. She has a smartphone but limited data, and finds most government sites confusing and English-only.

**How Siyani helps:** Onboards her in Xitsonga, learns her subjects and interests, and surfaces bursary and TVET options matched to her province and results — with a clear next action, not just a list of links.

## Persona 2 — Sipho, 27, Aspiring Entrepreneur

**Province:** Gauteng
**Status:** Employed part-time, wants to register a small business
**Home language:** isiZulu

Sipho has a business idea and some savings but has never registered a company and doesn't know which government funding programmes exist for first-time entrepreneurs.

**How Siyani helps:** Walks him through business registration steps in plain language and surfaces funding opportunities relevant to his sector and province.

## Persona 3 — Dr. Thandiwe Nkosi, 45, Provincial Government Liaison

**Province:** KwaZulu-Natal
**Status:** Works in a provincial digital-services office, evaluating citizen-facing tools

**How Siyani helps (partner perspective):** Thandiwe needs a channel that reliably reaches citizens in their own language without her department building and maintaining it. She evaluates Siyani on data privacy (POPIA alignment), accuracy, and whether it reduces or increases enquiry volume to her office.
