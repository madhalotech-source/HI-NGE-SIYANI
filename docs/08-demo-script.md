# Judging Demo Script

**Target length:** 4–5 minutes live demo + buffer for questions

## 1. Open on the problem (30 seconds)

> "Bursaries, government funding, business registration — the help exists, but it's scattered across websites most people never find, in a language many don't use every day. HI NGE SIYANI fixes that with one assistant: Siyani."

## 2. Language selection (20 seconds)

- Open the app, show the language screen.
- Select **Xitsonga** live. Narrate: "This isn't a translated skin — the whole conversation continues in this language."

## 3. Onboarding (30 seconds)

- Enter name, age, province, status ("Matric graduate").
- Narrate: "Siyani builds context once, so the person never has to repeat themselves."

## 4. Focus area selection (20 seconds)

- Select **Education** and **Government Services** together.
- Narrate: "One journey, not two separate apps."

## 5. Core guidance flow (90 seconds)

- Ask: "I want to apply for a bursary."
- Show Siyani asking a clarifying question (field of study / province) — narrate this explicitly: "Notice it asks rather than assumes — that's deliberate."
- Show the resulting personalised guidance/action plan.

## 6. Trust moment — the confidence fallback (30 seconds)

- Deliberately trigger an ambiguous or low-confidence input in a lower-resource language.
- Show Siyani's clarification or English fallback offer.
- Narrate: "Sepedi, Tshivenda, and Xitsonga have less AI language support today than English. Instead of guessing and misleading someone, Siyani says so — that's a trust feature, not a limitation we're hiding."

## 7. Roadmap close (30 seconds)

- Show the Product Roadmap slide/section: "This is what's working today. Finance, Healthcare, and the remaining official languages are next, clearly labelled Coming Soon — we're not overselling where we are."

## 8. Close (10 seconds)

> "Hi nge Siyani — no one gets left behind. Thank you, happy to take questions."

## Anticipated Judge Questions & Prepared Answers

- **"How accurate is it in Sepedi/Tshivenda/Xitsonga today?"** → Point to the confidence-fallback design; be honest that these languages have less mature AI support than English/isiZulu.
- **"What happens with government data/PII?"** → Point to [Security Strategy](16-security-strategy.md): minimal collection, consent-first, POPIA-aligned, user-controlled deletion.
- **"Why Delphi instead of Flutter/React Native?"** → It's the team's strongest, fastest-to-ship technology today; Flutter is a planned Phase 2 migration, not a gap being hidden.
