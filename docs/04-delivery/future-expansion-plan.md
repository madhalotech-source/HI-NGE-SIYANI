# Future Expansion Plan

Everything in this document is **Coming Soon**. None of it is part of the hackathon MVP claim.

## Platform Expansion

| Platform | Plan |
|---|---|
| Flutter (cross-platform) | Proof-of-concept alongside Delphi once Android MVP is stable, targeting eventual iOS parity |
| Web | React-based client sharing the existing Supabase/Firebase backend |
| iOS | Delivered via the Flutter migration, not a separate native build |

## Language Expansion

Beyond the initial five (English, isiZulu, Sepedi, Tshivenda, Xitsonga), expansion to the remaining official South African languages — Afrikaans, isiXhosa, Setswana, siSwati, isiNdebele, Tshivenda *(already included)*, and Xitsonga *(already included)* — will proceed based on demonstrated AI language-model support quality, not a fixed calendar date.

## AI & Infrastructure Expansion

- Python-based microservice for opportunity-matching and recommendation scoring
- Expanded confidence-scoring models per language, retrained as more usage data becomes available
- Multi-region backend deployment for national-scale reliability

## Partnership Expansion

- Formal integrations with provincial digital-services departments
- NGO and university partnerships for verified opportunity data feeds
- B2B licensing of the guidance engine to third parties (banks, youth programmes)

## Governing Principle

No expansion item moves from "Coming Soon" to the Product Roadmap's "Working" column until it is genuinely functional and tested — not merely prototyped.
