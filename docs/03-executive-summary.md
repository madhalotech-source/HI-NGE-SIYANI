# Executive Summary

## HI NGE SIYANI — Building Together. Rising Together.
*No one gets left behind.*

**Developed by:** MAD HALO Technologies
**Category:** Multilingual AI-powered civic & opportunity access platform

## The Problem

Opportunities in South Africa — bursaries, TVET pathways, government grants, business funding — are scattered across disconnected websites, offices, and queues. Most digital services assume English fluency, sidelining citizens who think and transact in another official language. Search engines return links, not a plan tailored to a person's age, province, and life stage.

## The Solution

HI NGE SIYANI introduces **Siyani**, a multilingual AI companion that holds one continuous conversation with a citizen, understands their context (age, province, current status), and builds a personalised action plan across the life areas they care about — instead of routing them to separate apps and portals.

## MVP Scope

The hackathon MVP focuses on two verticals, proven deeply before the platform widens:

- **Education** — bursary matching, TVET/university guidance, career direction
- **Government Services** — funding guidance, business registration walkthroughs, plain-language process explanations

Supported languages at MVP: **English, isiZulu, Sepedi, Tshivenda, Xitsonga** — with a built-in confidence-check fallback so Siyani asks for clarification rather than guessing in lower-resource languages.

## Technology

Built on technology the founding team can execute with confidence and speed: **Delphi 12 FMX** (Android client), **Supabase** (PostgreSQL + Auth), **Firebase** (real-time messaging/notifications), REST APIs, and **Git/GitHub** for version control. Full detail in [Software Architecture](04-architecture.md).
