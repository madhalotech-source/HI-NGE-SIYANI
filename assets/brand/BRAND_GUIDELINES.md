# Brand Guidelines

## Logo

`hi-nge-siyani-logo.png` (in this folder) — the official wordmark: "HI NGE" in black, "SIYANI" in green, with a green interlocking S / upward-arrow mark symbolising rising together.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Siyani Green | `#28893A` | "SIYANI" wordmark, icon, primary accent, CTAs |
| Ink Black | `#181818` | "HI NGE" wordmark, body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, text on dark/green surfaces |

## Wordmark Usage

- Always render as **"HI NGE SIYANI"** (with the space), matching the logo — not "HI NGE-SIYANI" or "HiNgeSiyani".
- "HI NGE" in Ink Black, "SIYANI" in Siyani Green, consistent with the logo.

## Slogan & Meaning

- **Primary slogan (always shown):** "Building Together. Rising Together."
- **Meaning (shown as supporting line where space allows):** "No one gets left behind."

## Typography

The logo uses a bold, geometric sans-serif. For documents and decks, a clean sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, or similar) keeps consistency without requiring a licensed font purchase.

## Do Not

- Do not recolor the wordmark outside Siyani Green / Ink Black without a documented reason.
- Do not stretch or distort the logo mark.
- Do not place the logo on low-contrast backgrounds (e.g. mid-grey).
